Job Market Paper

Farmers Facing Risk: Evidence from Ethanol Plant Expansion in the Midwest

Job Market Paper · Solo-authored

A growing literature attributes declining farm numbers and increasing average farm size to crop-insurance expansion. I argue that these patterns may instead reflect underlying price risk and heterogeneous risk aversion. Using a representative-farmer model and plausibly exogenous local corn-price exposure generated by ethanol-plant expansion, I estimate how price risk affects farm turnover and agricultural land markets.

Research Program

Risk, land use, and agricultural transition

My work connects three areas of agricultural and environmental economics.

Agricultural risk

Farm structure and land markets

I study how price volatility, insurance, and climate risk influence farm exit, consolidation, acreage, and agricultural land values.

Environmental quality

Land use and water resources

I estimate how agricultural production systems and environmental regulations affect nitrogen pollution, groundwater, and producer adaptation.

Farm transition

Stewardship before exit

I examine whether shortened planning horizons alter crop rotations and conservation incentives as producers approach land transfer.

Sustainable systems

Grazing adoption and rural welfare

I study how producer learning, social norms, and transitional technologies support economically viable pasture-based livestock systems.
