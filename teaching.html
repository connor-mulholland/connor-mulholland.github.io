Teaching

I teach environmental economics, natural resource economics, macroeconomics, and econometrics, with an emphasis on connecting economic models to applied policy questions.

Instructor of Record

  • ECON 333: Environmental and Natural Resource EconomicsWinter 2025; Winter 2026

    Designed and delivered the full undergraduate course covering externalities, public goods, benefit–cost analysis, renewable and nonrenewable resources, pollution regulation, climate policy, and conservation.

  • ECON 434: Advanced Environmental EconomicsSummer 2025; Fall 2026

    Designed a calculus-based course using optimization and applied models to analyze pollution control, resource extraction, renewable resources, climate change, and policy-instrument choice.

  • ECON 202: Introduction to MacroeconomicsSummer 2024

    Served as sole instructor and designed lectures, assignments, examinations, and course administration.
