Extension & Outreach

Applied research for agricultural decision-making

My Extension work translates agricultural economics, farm financial analysis, and agronomic knowledge into practical tools for producers, advisers, and conservation professionals.

Decision Support Tools

Working with the Grassland 2.0 Initiative and the University of Wisconsin–Madison Center for Integrated Agricultural Systems (CIAS), I co-developed two producer-focused decision support tools that help farmers evaluate the financial implications of transitioning from confinement to pasture-based livestock systems.

  • Heifer Grazing Compass
    An Excel-based decision support tool that evaluates the costs, labor requirements, land needs, and long-run financial outcomes of raising dairy replacement heifers on pasture.
    Visit the Heifer Grazing Compass →

  • Beef Grazing Compass
    A companion decision support tool designed to help beef producers evaluate the economics of pasture-based beef production, including grazing management, land requirements, labor, and profitability.
    Visit the Beef Grazing Compass →
