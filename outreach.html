Extension & Outreach

Applied research for agricultural decision-making

My Extension work translates agricultural economics, farm financial analysis, and agronomic knowledge into practical tools for producers, advisers, and conservation professionals.

Grassland 2.0

Heifer Grazing Compass

Co-developed a farm-level decision-support tool comparing confinement and grazing scenarios using operation-specific assumptions about feed, labor, land, infrastructure, animal performance, and management.

Grassland 2.0

Beef Grazing Compass

Co-developed an applied financial tool to help beef producers and advisers evaluate the management and profitability implications of grazing-based production systems.

Selected outreach and training

  • USDA NRCS and FSA training

    Delivered live training to more than 50 agricultural service providers on using the Compass tools in producer consultations.

  • World Dairy Expo

    Presented the Heifer Grazing Compass and operation-specific financial results to dairy producers and industry stakeholders.

  • GrassWorks Annual Grazing Conference

    Presented applied financial analysis and decision-support resources for grazing-based livestock production.

  • Public training resources

    Developed instructional materials supporting continued use of the tools by producers, conservation professionals, and advisers.
