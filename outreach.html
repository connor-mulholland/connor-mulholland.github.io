Extension & Outreach

Applied research for agricultural decision-making

My Extension work translates agricultural economics, farm financial analysis, and agronomic knowledge into practical tools for producers, advisers, and conservation professionals.

Decision Support Tools

Working with the Grassland 2.0 Initiative and the University of Wisconsin–Madison Center for Integrated Agricultural Systems, I co-developed two producer-focused tools that help livestock farmers evaluate the financial and management implications of pasture-based production.

Heifer Grazing Compass

A farm-level decision-support tool that helps dairy producers compare the costs, labor requirements, land needs, and financial implications of raising replacement heifers on pasture rather than in confinement.

View Heifer Grazing Compass

Beef Grazing Compass

A companion decision-support tool that helps beef producers assess grazing management, land requirements, labor needs, production costs, and the profitability of pasture-based beef systems.
